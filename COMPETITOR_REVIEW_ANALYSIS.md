# Competitor Review Mining — Ranked Wish-List & Build Priorities

Compiled October 31, 2026. Sources: Trustpilot (Rezi 129–233, Jobscan ~344, Teal ~93–116, Kickresume 2,202–3,631, Big Interview, Final Round AI ~255), G2 (Big Interview 12, Yoodli 8), Sitejabber/SmartCustomer (Jobscan, 1,699), Apple App Store (Huru), Reddit (r/Rezi, r/interviewing, r/publicspeaking), plus tested-review roundups.

**Source-quality caveat:** several harsh quotes come from competitor marketing blogs (wobo.ai, copilotinterview.com, remotejobassistant.com, rainaiservices.com, resumly.ai). Those have an incentive to amplify rivals' negatives. Rankings below weight complaints that appear **independently** across multiple platforms and in **first-person user quotes**; single-source claims are marked ⚠.

## 2. Evidence by theme

### #1 Billing, refunds, cancellation — universal and severe
- **Jobscan:** post-cancellation charges ("I have cancelled my subscription 3x and after a few days, they would still charge!"), charge attempt inside a 7-day trial, 2-day refund window with a 3.5% fee, BBB **C-**, Sitejabber **3.2/5 across 1,699 reviews**. ([SmartCustomer](https://www.smartcustomer.com/reviews/jobscan.co), [Wobo](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/jobscan-review/))
- **Rezi:** "I have been charged multiple times… I'm not even sure what all the charges are for"; the cancellation flow is called out as hard; a "free" site that asked $3 to download. ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/rezi.io?page=10), [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Rezi/comments/16fmori/rezi_review/))
- **Kickresume:** refund refusals despite an advertised 14-day guarantee (fine print: first-time subscribers only); cancellation is email-only; auto-renewal surprises. 4% of 3,585 Trustpilot reviews are one-star and they cluster here. ([RemoteJobAssistant](https://www.remotejobassistant.com/blog/kickresume-review), [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/kickresume.com?page=91))
- **Teal:** charges after cancellation; refund window contradicts the site (14 days advertised, 2 days for the weekly plan). ([ResumeJudge](https://resumejudge.com/blog/tealhq-review/), [RemoteJobAssistant](https://www.remotejobassistant.com/blog/teal-resume-review))
- **Big Interview:** unexpected $79 renewal at **0% logged usage**, only a half refund. ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.biginterview.com))
- **Final Round AI:** 17–18% one-star; **18% of reviewers use "scam" or "fraud"**; $288–$488 auto-renewals; no visible cancel button; monthly plans non-refundable. ([InterviewCoder](https://www.interviewcoder.co/blog/final-round-ai-review), [Rain AI analysis](https://rainaiservices.com/reviews/final-round-ai/))
- **Yoodli:** no refunds on cancellation.

> **Wish, verbatim:** "If they believe in their product and aren't just money grabbing they'd be confident sending a renew notification." — Jobscan reviewer, May 2026

### #2 Generic AI that ignores the user's actual experience
- **Rezi:** skills explorer suggested "group senthil" and "akash ztek"; a summary rewrite produced literally no change; AI "occasionally generates unrealistic suggestions or overly generic bullet points." ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/rezi.io?page=10))
- **Teal:** inserted "work authorization" as a professional skill in a headline; misspelled users' own names in ~half of cover letters; "very general and generic — it could have been on anyone's resume." ([RemoteJobAssistant](https://www.remotejobassistant.com/blog/teal-resume-review))
- **Kickresume:** writes from the job *title*, not the work history; "tacked AI into their existing interface"; output "too wordy for resume entries"; recruiters recognize it as AI. ([Wobo](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/kickresume-review), [SoundCV](https://www.soundcv.com/blog/kickresume-review))
- **Jobscan:** "It's suggestions made my resume word salad." ([Wobo](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/jobscan-review/))
- **Final Round AI:** "generic, not context-aware… you cannot really train the AI beforehand." ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/finalroundai.com?page=2))
- **Big Interview:** "the model replies in the app wasn't so useful for my field as an architect." ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/big-interview/reviews?order=most_recent&page=2))

### #3 Scores that don't mean what they imply
- **Jobscan:** match rate is keyword overlap, not ATS behaviour; users scored 98% and were rejected within an hour; inconsistent rescans by a hiring manager. 75% is the company's *recommendation*, not a validated threshold. ([Wobo](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/jobscan-review/), [CareerY](https://careery.pro/blog/resume-applications/is-jobscan-worth-it-2026), [CVLint](https://cvlint.com/blog/is-jobscan-worth-it))
- **Rezi:** relevant IT Engineer roles matched at 20/100; suggested "Finance Manager." ([Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Rezi/hot/))
- **Yoodli:** "great at analyzing how you speak… doesn't always evaluate the quality or strategy of what you're saying"; "I really wish there were more quantitative data points in the reports." ([Evro](https://www.evro.ai/resources/yoodli-review-2026), [InterviewFlow](https://interviewflowai.com/compare/yoodli))
- **Big Interview:** "graded my eye contact, not my LP answers." ([FR community](https://www.finalroundai.com/community/t/big-interview-graded-my-eye-contact-not-my-lp-answers/226))
- **Huru:** openly discloses that speech recognition "can occasionally mishear a word" and that its scores are "coaching signals, not hiring decisions" — the best handling found in the category, and a model to copy. ([Huru](https://huru.ai/about/), [CoPilot](https://copilotinterview.com/blog/huru-vs-yoodli))

### #4–5 Export fidelity and real ATS safety
- **Rezi:** "The exported version of the resume looks NOTHING like the version in the app."
- **Kickresume:** DOCX export "total garbage" — bullets collapsed into run-ons, columns merged, headers gone; 40 minutes to rebuild; PDF is the only reliable output.
- **Jobscan:** Power Edit forces their templates (several not ATS-ingestible); formatting breaks on download; three hours of edits lost to a save error.
- **Teal:** two-column templates parse incorrectly in Workday-type ATS (Tom's Guide, via RemoteJobAssistant).

### #6–7 Free-tier design and usage transparency
- **Rezi:** built a resume free, then $3 demanded at download; "I was told this was a free site."
- **Kickresume:** free tier capped at 4 templates, **2 work entries, 2 skills** — not submittable; ATS checker paywalled; paying users blocked "the second day of my new bill cycle… I only made 16 resumes," with no usage meter or warning.
- **Teal:** "free" but 10 bullet credits / 2 summary / 2 cover-letter credits evaporate in one session; unused credits never roll over.
- **Jobscan:** 5 scans per 30 days; trial locks most features.
- **Big Interview:** no free tier at all, yet appears on "free interview tools" lists.

### #8 Reliability and data loss
- **Jobscan:** month-long outage; "no matter which resumes I request… Jobscan always refers to an old resume already deleted from the system."
- **Teal:** user locked out and "directed to a new account instead of my old one, which I spent several days and hours building."
- **Rezi:** "locked out of my account until 5 May."

### #10 Fragmentation — the structural opening
- "No interview preparation tools are included in Teal… you'll need a separate tool." ([Awesome Agents](https://awesomeagents.ai/tools/best-ai-resume-writers-2026/))
- Big Interview has only "a basic ATS scoring tool" for resumes. ([FinalRound blog](https://www.finalroundai.com/blog/big-interview-review-pros-cons))
- Rezi is resume-only; Yoodli is delivery-only; Jobscan is scanning-only.

### #12 Audience mismatch
- Big Interview: "introductory for experienced candidates" (I Got An Offer, via [MockInterviewPro](https://www.mockinterviewpro.com/reviews/big-interview)); architect quoted above; Rezi reviewer asking for a military-service section.

## 3. Build priorities for ProReady (derived)

### Must fix before charging money
1. **PDF/DOCX export that matches the preview.** Your current download is HTML. Complaint #4 is the most predictable one-star review you will receive.
2. **One-click cancel + a stated, honored refund window.** No email chain, no bot. Make this a visible feature, not fine print.
3. **A plain-language "what this score measures" note on every number.** e.g. *"This is a 0–100 practice signal built from structure, evidence and keyword match. It is not a prediction from any employer's system."* Copy Huru's candor.
4. **A visible "here's what's free" card up front** — what you get for $0, in full, before any work is invested.
5. **A single-column, plain-text-first export path** labeled "maximum ATS compatibility," and say why.

### Build soon (differentiators competitors won't copy)
6. **One-click backup / restore of local data.** Your on-device design is a privacy win and a data-loss risk. A JSON export kills the #8 complaint.
7. **Real usage meter** if you ever meter anything — never a silent wall.
8. **Keep the proofreader as the wedge.** Nobody else in this set gives prioritized, line-by-line feedback on the resume someone already has. It answers complaint #2 directly: it improves *their* words instead of inventing a stranger's.

### Deliberately skip
9. **Auto-apply.** Most-desired missing feature (#15), biggest legal/reputational trap, and every competitor that chased volume is now drowning in billing complaints.
10. **Weekly billing.** Best LTV in the data, worst reputation in this category of users.
