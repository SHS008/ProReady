      Step 3 · Prep School

      Know the rules before you walk in

      Short, practical lessons for professional settings — offices, healthcare, banking, government, customer-facing and supervisor roles.

      The Professional Do's & Don'ts

      ✅ DO

      • Arrive 10–15 min early; silence your phone
      • Firm handshake (or friendly nod), smile, eye contact
      • Research the company: what they do, who they serve
      • Bring 2 resume copies, a pen, and your questions list
      • Use examples (STAR) instead of "I'm a hard worker"
      • Thank them, ask about next steps, send a thank-you

      🚫 DON'T

      • Bad-mouth a former employer — ever
      • Answer with just "yes/no" or ramble past ~2 minutes
      • Ask about pay/time off in the first minutes
      • Chew gum, wear a hat, or bring a drink in
      • Say "I have no questions"
      • Lie or exaggerate — it surfaces in background checks
      👔 What to wear: One step more formal than the daily dress code. Clean, pressed slacks/skirt, button-down or blouse, closed-toe shoes. Healthcare admin: business casual, minimal fragrance. When unsure, it's okay to ask the scheduler: "What is the dress code?"
      🧍 Body language: Sit tall, feet flat, hands visible. Nod while listening. Look at the person asking, then include others. Nervous? Slow breathing + a sip of water (before you go in) helps.
      🎥 Video interviews: Camera at eye level, light in front of you, quiet room, plain background, dress fully professional, test 30 min early, look at the camera when answering.

          Step 5 · Day-of & follow-up

          Finish strong

          Small professional touches after the interview often tip close decisions.

          🌙 Night Before

          • Lay out outfit, check for stains/wrinkles, polish shoes
          • Print 2 resume copies + note interviewer names & address
          • Plan route/parking or test video link & camera
          • Review the posting, your STAR stories, and your 3 questions for them
          • Set two alarms; aim for real sleep

          ☀️ Day Of — Bring

          • Photo ID, resume copies, pen & small notebook
          • Certifications/licenses list & references (names, titles, phone/email)
          • Phone silenced before you walk in
          • Arrive 10–15 min early; be kind to everyone (reception counts!)

          Tough moments, handled with class

          "You don't have a degree / direct experience."

          "That's right, my background is in ___, where I built skills in ___ and ___. I completed ___ training, and I'm eager to apply that here. In my last role I…" Then give a STAR example.

          Employment gap

          Be brief, honest, forward-looking: "From 2022–2024 I cared for a family member full-time. During that time I ___. I'm now fully available and excited to return to…"

          Salary question

          "I'm focused on finding the right fit. Could you share the budgeted range for this role?" Research local ranges beforehand (e.g., Michigan Works / Bureau of Labor Statistics).

          "Why did you leave your last job?"

          Neutral & forward: "I learned a lot there. I'm looking for ___ (growth, schedule stability, work that uses my ___ skills), which is why this role stood out."
