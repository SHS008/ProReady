Privacy Policy — ProReady Coach

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Contact: [REPLACE WITH YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL BEFORE PUBLISHING]

The short version: ProReady Coach is designed to work on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your resume, your interview answers, your video, or your voice from this app. We do not sell data, run advertising trackers, or build profiles.

1. Information the app handles — on your device

The content you type or paste into the app (target job, job posting, resume builder entries, resumes submitted for proofreading, STAR stories, thank-you drafts, and mock-interview transcripts and scores) is stored only in your browser/app storage on your device so your work is still there when you return. It is not transmitted to us. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's stored data (browser site data, or the app's storage in your device settings) or by deleting the app.

2. Camera and microphone

The mock-interview feature asks permission to use your camera and microphone so you can see yourself and answer aloud. Video is displayed to you only; it is not recorded, stored, or transmitted by the app. Microphone audio is used only to produce a transcript of your answer on screen (see Section 3). You may decline either permission and still use the app by typing.

3. Speech features

The interviewer's spoken questions use your device's built-in text-to-speech. If you answer by voice, transcription uses your platform's speech-recognition service (for example, your browser's or operating system's speech service). Depending on that service, audio may be processed by the platform provider (such as Apple or Google) under their privacy policy to convert speech to text. The resulting transcript is handled as described in Section 1. Typing your answers avoids speech services entirely.

4. Files you open

Resume files you select for proofreading are read and analyzed locally on your device. They are not uploaded.

5. No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking

The app has no user accounts and contains no advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no cross-app tracking.

6. Children

ProReady Coach is intended for job seekers and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Downloads you create

Resumes and reports you choose to download or share are saved wherever you direct your device to save them. What you do with those files afterward (for example, uploading a resume to a job board) is governed by that service's policies.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date. Questions: [REPLACE WITH YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL BEFORE PUBLISHING].
